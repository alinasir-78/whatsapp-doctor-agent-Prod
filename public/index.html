⏰ AI 24-Hour Confirmation & Attendance Engine — Functionality Guide

Purpose & Clinical Objective:

Patient no-shows cost medical practices an estimated $150B annually. This engine automates patient attendance verification 24 hours prior to their appointment, allowing clinics to protect scheduled revenue, notify doctors, and reclaim unconfirmed slots for waiting patients.

🚀 Button 1: "Run 24h Confirmation Scan"

Scans the appointment roster for tomorrow's date (within 24 to 48 hours). For every appointment where an automated confirmation ping has not yet been sent:

  • WhatsApp Outreach Dispatched: Sends an interactive WhatsApp reminder to the patient's mobile number with appointment time, doctor name, and instructions.
  • Response Options Provided: The patient is asked to reply with CONFIRM (keeps booking confirmed), RESCHEDULE (triggers conversational AI rescheduling), or CANCEL (frees the slot).
  • Timestamp Recorded: Sets confirmation_sent_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP in the appointment record to prevent duplicate reminders.
  • Doctor Notification: Sends an alert copy to the attending physician's personal WhatsApp number and logs it in the Doctor Alerts feed.
⚠️ Button 2: "Mark Unconfirmed as Tentative"

Simulates the 24-hour response cutoff. For appointments scheduled for tomorrow where a confirmation message was sent but the patient has not yet responded:

  • Status Transition: Switches the appointment status from Confirmed to Tentative.
  • Visual Calendar Alert: The slot in the Weekly Calendar immediately turns yellow with a pulsating indicator, alerting the physician and front desk of attendance risk.
  • Physician Phone Alert: Dispatches a real-time alert to the assigned doctor: "Appointment marked TENTATIVE due to lack of client confirmation response."
  • Front Desk Action: Enables reception to call the patient or offer the slot to waitlist and walk-in patients if the patient fails to show.
🔄 Automated Production Execution (No Button Needed):

In production, a background CRON daemon runs POST /api/reminders/run every hour automatically. These buttons allow clinic owners, directors, and receptionists to manually trigger immediate scans and test timeout workflows on demand.
